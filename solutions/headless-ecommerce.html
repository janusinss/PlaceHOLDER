Engineering Rationale

Architectural Trade-Off Matrix

Deliberate technology selections evaluated on operational complexity, infrastructure cost, and developer velocity.

Storefront Architecture

Sub-second page speeds globally and complete bespoke design freedom.
Chosen Implementation
Headless Next.js on Edge CDN
Alternative Evaluated
Monolithic Liquid / WordPress WooCommerce templates

Decouples the frontend presentation layer from backend commerce logic, preventing database lockups during flash sale traffic spikes.

Cart & Inventory State

Zero cart lag and drastically lower abandonment rates.
Chosen Implementation
Optimistic Client Cart with Server Webhook Inventory Verification
Alternative Evaluated
Synchronous database cart lookups on every click

Provides instant feedback when shoppers add items to cart, reserving inventory only at the checkout threshold.

Product Catalog Search

Instantaneous search results on mobile devices.
Chosen Implementation
Dedicated Algolia / Meilisearch Indexing
Alternative Evaluated
Native SQL database wildcard queries

Provides sub-25ms multi-attribute filtering (size, color, price) without burdening the primary commerce database.

Technical Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Deep technical answers regarding integration, scale, and operational handover.

Can we still use Shopify as our backend while having a headless Next.js frontend?

Yes. This is the gold standard modern setup. You continue using Shopify for inventory, order fulfillment, shipping, and payment gateways, while MIHA Technologies builds a custom Next.js frontend that loads 3x faster than standard Shopify themes.

How do marketing teams manage promotional banners and content without coding?

We integrate headless CMS platforms like Sanity.io or Strapi. Marketing teams can change copy, images, promotional banners, and SEO metadata visually with real-time preview.

How do you handle traffic spikes during Black Friday or flash sales?

Because the storefront is pre-rendered and served from Cloudflare / Vercel Edge networks, 95% of requests never touch the database. Your site remains 100% responsive under hundreds of thousands of concurrent visitors.
