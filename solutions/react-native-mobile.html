Technical Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Deep technical answers regarding integration, scale, and operational handover.

Can React Native genuinely achieve the same performance as native Swift and Kotlin?

Yes. With the New Architecture and Hermes engine, React Native executes compiled native C++ code directly without bridge bottlenecks, achieving 60/120 FPS parity on modern iOS and Android devices.

How do Over-The-Air (OTA) updates work without App Store review?

Apple and Google guidelines permit updating JavaScript bundles and assets dynamically as long as the core functionality matches your approved store listing, enabling same-day bug fixes.

Do you assist with the Apple App Store and Google Play review approval?

Yes. We handle privacy manifests, store metadata, screenshot generation, and respond directly to review inquiries until your app is officially approved and live.
